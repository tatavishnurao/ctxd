# Travel and expense policy

Fictional sample content for the ctxd dashboard demo.

## What the company pays for

The company pays for reasonable costs that are needed for work: business travel, customer meals, approved training, conference tickets and home office equipment within the yearly allowance. It does not pay for personal entertainment, traffic or parking fines, alcohol outside customer meals, or upgrades to first class.

## Approval before you spend

Expenses under 100 dollars need no approval in advance. Anything from 100 to 1,000 dollars needs approval from your manager before you spend. Anything above 1,000 dollars, and all international travel, also needs approval from the department head. Approve in the expense tool so the decision is recorded.

## Booking travel

Book flights, trains and hotels through the company travel portal. Choose economy class for flights under six hours and premium economy for longer flights. Book at least fourteen days ahead when you can; late bookings need a short reason. Hotels should be within the city rate limit shown in the portal.

## Meals and per diem

When you travel overnight, you receive a daily meal allowance instead of claiming each meal. The allowance depends on the city and is shown in the travel portal. Customer meals are claimed separately with the names of the attendees and the business purpose. Team celebrations need approval from the manager who owns the team budget.

## Receipts

Keep an itemized receipt for every expense above 25 dollars. Take a photo of the receipt in the expense app on the same day; paper receipts do not need to be kept after the photo is uploaded. If a receipt is lost, fill in the missing receipt form; more than two missing receipts in a year are reviewed by finance.

## Corporate card

Employees who travel more than four times a year receive a corporate card. Use it only for business expenses. Each card transaction must be matched to a receipt and a purpose within thirty days. Personal charges made by mistake are reported at once and paid back within thirty days.

## Submitting a claim and reimbursement

Submit claims in the expense app within thirty days of the expense. Group the expenses of one trip into one report. Finance reviews claims every week and pays approved claims with the next payroll. Claims submitted more than ninety days late are not paid unless the department head approves an exception.

## Mileage

When you use your own car for business travel, claim mileage at the standard rate published by finance each January. Record the start point, the end point, the distance and the business purpose. Travel between home and your usual office is commuting and cannot be claimed.
